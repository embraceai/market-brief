# Market Brief data

Generated twice a day (06:00 and 11:00) by `brief/generator/brief.mjs` from the X Lists research
(Must-know posts and Setups to research). Read by the Market Brief phone app.

- `index.json`: the list of briefs, newest first
- `briefs/<YYYY-MM-DD-HHMM>.json`: one brief (stories, detail cards, ticker prices)

Aggregated from posts and web research. Not investment advice.
